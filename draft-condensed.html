Jackup overmanning

Modest jackup overmanning, a direct ~2% gross-margin drag at the bonus-loaded jackup expat rate. ~$0.8–1.0M annual recovery per rig.

Land · critical combination

MIDIR & DRAVUS — overmanning + expat overload.

16% / 11% expat-cost vs land norm <8%. Workforce >35% of revenue can't reach the 40% profitability floor; MIDIR sits at 51.9% — structurally unprofitable at current dayrate.

Org evolution · 7-rig steady state

Build dedicated Supply Chain, Assets & Maintenance competencies.

Strengthen three functional competencies the fleet currently lacks: Supply Chain (ME2N/MB51/MB52, GR/IR, country-base strategy), Assets (register, transfers, certification) and Maintenance (CMMS, PM compliance, OEM routines). Today these skills are bundled / procurement sits under Finance.

Recommendation · Land Rigs — Strategic Business-Model Review

MIDIR & DRAVUS need a business-model review.

The land segment is loss-making (MIDIR) or sub-par (DRAVUS) despite peer-level CAPEX. The problem is structural — manning, cost base, rig spec and capital all need to be on the table. A complete overhaul, not a line-by-line trim.

SCOPE OF REVIEW
  • Review manning & POB vs land-rig norms
  • Review workforce cost structure — exceeding 50% of theoretical revenue
  • Review rig spec, sizing & capabilities vs the work won
  • Review CAPEX deployment & payback
DECISIONS ON THE TABLE
  • Upgrade to a more competitive spec
  • Re-deploy to better-fit work
  • Replace or exit rigs that cannot earn their capital back
  • Restructure crew model — expat ratio & rotation
